# OUMATRA Brand Foundation

Version 1 · Step 1

## Name

**OUMATRA**

The brand name is always written in uppercase in formal brand treatments. Normal sentence-case usage may be considered later as part of a broader editorial system; it is not established here.

## Tagline

**From Sound. Beyond Silence.**

The punctuation and wording are part of the locked tagline.

## Name origin

OUMATRA is conceptually derived from:

- **OM** — primordial sound, origin, and creation.
- **Amatra** — the silence beyond OM.

Together, the name represents the journey from origin to creation, from sound to silence, and from potential to enduring reality.

## Company purpose

OUMATRA exists to build, nurture, and own enduring category-defining companies across technology and future industries.

## Positioning

OUMATRA is a global parent/holding company. Its identity is deliberately industry-neutral and designed to accommodate a family of companies over time.

OUMATRA must not be positioned narrowly as an AI company, software company, consulting firm, startup studio, or agency. Individual OUMATRA companies may operate in specific categories, but the parent brand represents the enduring foundation connecting them.

## Mission

> To create and nurture enduring companies that transform meaningful ideas into products and institutions that improve everyday life.

**Status:** Approved for v1; intentionally easy to revise in a later brand-governance review.

## Vision

> To build a family of category-defining companies that endure for generations.

**Status:** Approved for v1; intentionally easy to revise in a later brand-governance review.

## Brand personality

OUMATRA should feel:

- Timeless
- Intelligent
- Calm
- Foundational
- Ambitious
- Human
- Premium without appearing luxurious for the sake of luxury
- Visionary without startup hype

Avoid generic futuristic AI aesthetics, excessive gradients, glowing neon, pervasive glassmorphism, crypto aesthetics, and overly corporate stock-template design. Black and gold are not the default brand expression.

## Voice and tone

The OUMATRA voice is assured, thoughtful, concise, and human.

- **Foundational, not grandiose:** Speak about enduring intent without unearned claims.
- **Visionary, not breathless:** Express possibility with calm conviction, not hype.
- **Intelligent, not opaque:** Prefer clear language and meaningful ideas over jargon.
- **Premium, not ornamental:** Let restraint, craft, and precision communicate quality.
- **Global, not generic:** Use inclusive language while preserving a distinct point of view.

Do not invent industries, performance claims, customers, revenue, investment activity, scale, or achievements.

## Colour system

| Token | Hex | Role guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Teal | `#0E3D4A` | Primary brand anchor; dark surfaces and primary text |
| Indigo | `#303A6B` | Secondary dark surface; depth and institutional calm |
| Plum | `#7B2E5B` | Expressive accent used with restraint |
| Warm Peach | `#D08A6A` | Human, warm accent and detail colour |
| Ivory | `#F3F0EB` | Primary light surface and text on dark brand surfaces |

These exact values are locked and implemented as reusable CSS and Tailwind tokens in `app/globals.css`. Components must reference tokens rather than repeat hex codes. Both light and dark brand surfaces should preserve readable contrast; palette colours are not automatically valid for every text/background pairing.

## Logo concept

The approved conceptual direction is a mark composed of opposing organic, crescent-like forms surrounding a central vertical sound-wave motif, with small upper and lower dots. It represents sound and silence.

The supplied brand-kit image is conceptual artwork, not a production SVG. It must not be traced and presented as the final master. Do not replace it with an infinity symbol, generic technology icon, letter O icon, religious symbol, crescent/star, architecture/building mark, or any generated symbol. Production asset locations are reserved under `public/brand/` and will remain empty until approved masters are supplied.
