# OUMATRA Brand Decisions

This decision register separates approved foundations from unresolved work. An item remains open until it is explicitly reviewed and moved to **Locked**; implementation convenience does not constitute brand approval.

## LOCKED

- **Name:** OUMATRA.
- **Tagline:** From Sound. Beyond Silence.
- **Conceptual origin:** OM (primordial sound, origin, and creation) + Amatra (the silence beyond OM).
- **Positioning:** A global parent/holding company created to build, nurture, and own enduring category-defining companies.
- **Palette:** Deep Teal `#0E3D4A`, Indigo `#303A6B`, Plum `#7B2E5B`, Warm Peach `#D08A6A`, and Ivory `#F3F0EB`.
- **Logo conceptual direction:** Opposing organic/crescent-like forms surrounding a central vertical sound-wave motif, with small upper/lower dots, representing sound and silence.
- **Identity scope:** Industry-neutral parent-company identity. OUMATRA is not narrowly positioned as an AI company, software company, consultancy, startup studio, or agency.

## OPEN

- Final production SVG geometry
- Final typeface selection
- Exact spacing and clear-space rules
- Monochrome logo variants
- Favicon final artwork
- Social avatar artwork
- Motion identity
- Full website design

## Working conventions that are not brand locks

The Step 1 preview uses system sans-serif and serif type stacks to avoid prematurely selecting a typeface. Its layout, spacing, responsive behavior, and editorial composition demonstrate direction only; they do not move any open item into the locked section.
